You can pay using the following methods

Online payment

Use our online payment platform with bank cards or a QR code

Campus cashier

You can pay on campus to your local campus cashier with bank cards .

Bank transfer

Transfer to the RMIT Vietnam Vietcombank account

Cash deposit or credit card

Directly deposit into the RMIT Vietnam Vietcombank account.

Online payment

Online payments provide you with secure payment, automatic confirmation of your payment and fast processing times. Expand all sections

RMIT payments Step 1

Go to https://payments.rmit.edu.vn Step 2

Select Fee Payment or Lump Sum Step 3

Provide your

Student ID (mandatory field)

Family name

Email (mandatory field)

State the amount that you are paying (mandatory) Step 4

Click Add to cart, and then Process Payment.

Click on Payment option, then click Continue.

You will notice that the information is sent to a payment gateway. Step 5

Choose the type of payment you will use to pay your tuition fees with and click “Pay Now”.

myRMIT

You may pay your fees via myRMIT , which students can access once you receive your student ID.

Once you log in, choose the Financial Account tile in myRMIT.

Vietcombank i-B@NKING (for Vietcombank account holders ONLY)

Step 1: Go to the Vietcombank website: https://www.vietcombank.com.vn/

Step 2: Log in → Payment → Bill payment

Step 3: Select service → Tuition fee → Select service provider → RMIT Vietnam - HCM campus or RMIT Vietnam - Hanoi campus

Step 4: Select fee type from the drop-down list → Fill in the payment amount → Fill in the Customer code (Student ID number or Birth Date only if Student ID is not issued) → Fill in Customer name (Student name) → Confirm

Step 5: Enter verification code → Confirm

Step 6: Enter OTP (Sent via SMS to the phone number registering with the bank)

Step 7: Confirm (successful transactions will be confirmed by SMS and email).

Note:

For Customer code: Enter correctly the Student ID Number with format: Sxxxxxxx or Lxxxxxxx (x: number). If you don’t have Student ID Number yet, enter your birthdate with the following format: xxyyzzzz (xx: date; yy: month; zzzz: year).

For Customer name: Enter full name of Student – NOT name of account owner if the account owner is not the student

Campus cashier

All campus cashiers accept bank cards only.

To assist new students, RMIT Vietnam makes an exception for the first transaction of tuition fee deposits to be made in cash*. Extra fees apply.

* First transactions with RMIT Vietnam of either a tuition fee deposit or an English course tuition fee

Bank transfer

When making a bank transfer, please leave a note on your transaction with details as follows:

Student name – Student ID number (or birth date if unavailable) – Payment details (like “Tuition Fee Semester 3”) Expand all sections
