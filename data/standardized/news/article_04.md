## Payment methods

The official RMIT Vietnam payment-methods page describes online payment, bank transfer, payment by bank card at a campus cashier, and cash deposit at Vietcombank. Online payment through the university platform can use a bank card or QR code and is presented as the recommended route. For bank payments, students must use the correct identifying information so the university can allocate the transaction to the right financial account. The instructions for Vietcombank bill payment require selection of the RMIT Vietnam campus and fee type, followed by the payment amount, customer code, and student name. The customer code is normally the student ID; where an ID has not yet been issued, the specified birth-date format may be used. Students should retain transaction confirmation and check myRMIT after processing.
