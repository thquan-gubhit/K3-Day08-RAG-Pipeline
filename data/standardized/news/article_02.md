## Tuition payment FAQ

RMIT advises students to check cleared payments through the Financial Account tile in myRMIT or ask Student Connect; clearing can take up to three days. The standard tuition payment deadline is Friday in week 3 of a semester, with different deadlines for block and intensive postgraduate courses. Missing the deadline can lead to withdrawal from enrolled courses and administrative cancellation unless an exceptional-circumstances request is lodged and approved on time. Students can pay through myRMIT, bank transfer, or credit card. The page explains that a negative account balance represents a credit or reversal and may be available for refund, re-credit, or offset against future fees. It also provides add/drop deadlines and medical-insurance guidance. Students should use the current guide for complete conditions.
